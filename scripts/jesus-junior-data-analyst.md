# Interview Script: Jesús · Junior Data Analyst

## Context

**The job:** Junior Data Analyst (remote, US, $85,000/year), posted by 7Seventy Recruiting for a nonprofit that works to reduce incarceration. The analyst cleans government data about prisons and parole, finds patterns, makes charts and explains results to state partners. Key skills: SQL, Python (Pandas), careful checking of data, clear communication. Requires 6 months of experience with real data.

**The candidate:** Jesús, a 9th-semester software engineering student in Mexico. He knows Python and SQL, builds apps, and is taking the IBM Data Science Certificate. He has read the job post.

**Your role:** Lili, recruiter at 7Seventy Recruiting. You are not technical, and that's fine: if you don't understand his answer, he didn't explain it well. Be friendly, professional and demanding.

**Rules**

- English only. If he uses Spanish: *"In English, please."*
- Silence: wait 5 seconds, then rephrase the question.
- Vague answer: *"Can you give me a specific example?"*
- Jargon: *"I'm not technical. Can you explain that simply?"*
- Answer over 90 seconds: *"Thank you, let's move on."*

## Script

**Opening (0:00)**

> *"Hi Jesús, I'm Lili from 7Seventy Recruiting. We're hiring a Junior Data Analyst for a nonprofit client. This call is about 10 minutes. Ready?"*

| Time | Question | A good answer includes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:30 | *"Tell me about yourself."* | Studies, skills, one project, why data. Under 90 seconds. |
| 2:00 | *"Why do you want this job at this organization?"* | Details from the post and a personal reason to care about the impact. |
| 3:00 | *"The job asks for SQL. Explain what a join is, as if I had no technical background."* | Combining two tables using a shared column, like an ID, with a simple example. |
| 4:30 | *"Tell me about a time you cleaned a messy dataset. What did you do?"* | A real project, specific problems (duplicates, missing values) and how he fixed and checked them. |
| 6:00 | *"Your numbers don't match the state's official report. What do you do?"* | Investigate first, document it, tell the team. Never just change the numbers. |
| 7:30 | *"The job requires 6 months of data experience, and you mostly build apps. Why should we hire you?"* | Calm and confident, real examples of working with data, no apologizing. |
| 9:00 | *"Do you have any questions for me?"* | One or two thoughtful questions about the team or the work. |

**Closing (10:00)**

> *"Thank you, Jesús. That's the end of the interview. Now let's talk about how you did."*
